First release. Nine modules: GSTIN (state code, embedded PAN, check digit), PAN (holder type), IFSC (offline parsing and an optional lookup with your own fetcher), UPI (VPA validation and upi://pay links), Indian mobile numbers (normalising, formatting, wa.me links), pincodes (zone and postal circle), Aadhaar (Verhoeff checksum and masking only), money (Indian grouping, lakh and crore short forms, words in English and Hindi, integer-paise arithmetic) and vehicle registration numbers (state and BH series). Zero dependencies, ESM and CJS builds with types, one entry point per module.
